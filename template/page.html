How this page stays uncluttered
  1. Fixed slots: each topic holds 1 pinned classic plus the 2 most recent qualifying reviews. Nothing else is displayed.
  2. New in, oldest out: when a new review qualifies, the oldest unpinned one retires automatically.
  3. Retired is one line: a count with a Restore link. Anything you have starred stays saved in this browser.
  4. No padding: a topic with fewer good reviews shows fewer.
  5. Quiet topics collapse: a topic with nothing new in 12 months folds into a Dormant row.
  6. Quality gate: PubMed review publication type, a curated journal list, the last 24 months. Pinned classics are the only exception.
  7. Authors' own words: abstracts and conclusions are the authors' text from PubMed; nothing on this page is AI-written.
